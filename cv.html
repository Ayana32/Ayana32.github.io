Research experience

MSc Dissertation Research — Cross-Lingual Transfer of Activation SteeringUniversity of Sheffield · Supervisor: Dr Cass Zhao · Feb 2026 – Sep 2026
  • Investigated the robustness of activation-level interventions in multilingual LLMs using Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA), testing whether English-derived steering vectors transfer consistently across three model families and five languages.
  • Identified a representation–behaviour gap: high steering-vector similarity did not reliably predict the magnitude or direction of behavioural transfer across models and tasks, concluding that high representational similarity is not sufficient to guarantee comparable behavioural effects, and leaving the question of necessity unresolved.
  • Designed layer-wise evaluations with bootstrap confidence intervals, held-out sets, and Gaussian and permutation random-vector baselines to characterise failure modes and distinguish systematic effects from noise.
  • Conducted a separate cross-task evaluation using English-derived truthfulness vectors on TruthfulQA, finding that transfer success reversed across models and behaviours: Qwen3.5-9B transferred sycophancy but not truthfulness, OLMo3-7B-Instruct the reverse.
