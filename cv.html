Research experience

MSc Dissertation Research — Multilingual LLMsUniversity of Sheffield · Supervisor: Dr Cass Zhao · Feb 2026 – Present
  • Investigated the reliability of representation-level interventions in multilingual LLMs, using Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) to test whether English-derived steering vectors transfer consistently across models and languages.
  • Quantified cross-lingual representational similarity between steering vectors (cosine) and found that high geometric similarity did not guarantee equivalent behavioural effects, showing that representational similarity does not by itself establish behavioural equivalence.
  • Designed evaluations to characterise when and where generalisation breaks down, using bootstrap confidence intervals, held-out sets, and Gaussian and permutation baselines to distinguish systematic effects from noise.
  • Extended the analysis to TruthfulQA and cross-task settings to test whether observed effects generalise beyond a single behaviour or benchmark.
