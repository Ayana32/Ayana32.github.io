MSc Dissertation · 2026

Cross-Lingual Activation Steering in LLMs

Evaluation languages: English, Italian, Chinese, Korean and Kazakh

Testing whether English-derived activation steering transfers across English, Italian, Chinese, Korean and Kazakh, and whether representational alignment predicts behavioural change.

Experiments run on HPC across three model families, with bootstrap confidence intervals and permutation baselines for behavioural comparisons.

Activation SteeringSycophancyTruthfulQA QwenGemmaOLMo
KEY FINDING Representation similarity ≠ behavioural reliability.

Cross-lingual steering-vector cosine similarity remained high (0.918–0.990), while behavioural effects varied substantially across languages and model families.

05 / HOW I THINK

I like turning ambiguous problems into structures we can test.

That pattern goes back to my undergraduate thesis, where I structured the question of organisational adaptation among Uzbekistani migrant workers in Korea into dimensions that could be analysed systematically. It continued through industrial engineering, data operations, sustainability consulting and now NLP research: I tend to break ambiguity into measurable components, test the assumptions behind them, and turn them into something we can reason about or act on.

In collaboration, I try to make uncertainty visible early: I write down assumptions, flag unresolved questions, and explain technical decisions clearly enough that others can challenge or build on them.
